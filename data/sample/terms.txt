RIVERSIDE SOUND FESTIVAL 2026 — TICKET TERMS AND CONDITIONS

7. Cancellations and refunds

7.1 If a festival day is cancelled, single-day tickets for that day will be refunded in full.

7.2 If any single festival day is cancelled, multi-day pass holders will receive a refund equal to that day's share of the pass price.

7.3 Pass holders who did not attend any other festival day may instead receive a full refund.

7.4 VIP hospitality packages ($100 per pass) are non-refundable.

7.5 Camping add-ons are refunded per night for any cancelled night.

7.6 Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 14 days of the announcement.

7.7 Bad weather alone does not entitle ticket holders to a refund unless a day is cancelled by the organizer.
